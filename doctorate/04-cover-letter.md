**Re: Application for admission — Doctor of Ministry**

I am writing to apply for admission to the Doctor of Ministry, and to ask the Graduate Research School to consider my application under the provision for applicants who demonstrate research capacity other than through a prior research thesis.

I want to be direct about my position at the outset. I hold a bachelor's degree without an honours thesis, and no masters. I therefore do not meet the standard academic prerequisite of a prior research thesis of at least 12,000 words. I also hold an honorary doctorate from Alphacrucis, and I am aware that an honorary award confers recognition rather than academic standing — I mention it here for completeness and I make no claim on it for admission purposes.

What I bring instead is this.

For twenty-six years I have led Futures Church Global, from a single declining congregation in Adelaide to a network of twenty-one campuses across Australia, the United States, Indonesia and Brazil. In the last twelve years we have planted more than twenty-one churches. Most flourished. At least one closed, as a direct result of a decision I made against our own stated criteria. I have served fourteen years as a national director across a movement of more than a thousand churches, and ten years as a state president. This is roughly forty years of practice against the Doctor of Ministry's five-year requirement.

In 2026 I published *Multiply or Die*, an 84,000-word book setting out what I believe I have learned about multiplication. It is a trade book. It is not peer-reviewed, it states no methodology, and it does not test its own claims — and it is those three facts, rather than the book's reception, that have led me to apply.

**My proposed research treats the book's propositions as hypotheses rather than conclusions.** I have asserted a set of laws about how church multiplication works, derived inductively from four decades of practice. Nobody, including me, has subjected them to systematic comparative scrutiny. I am in an unusual position to do so: I hold a complete organisational archive covering every plant the network has initiated since 2000, in four national contexts, including the ones that failed. A study that examines the full population of those plants — and that is designed so that finding my published claims wrong counts as a successful outcome — would, I believe, make a genuine contribution to a field whose evidence base is distorted by the reluctance to report failure.

I am equally aware that being the founder, the decision-maker and the published advocate makes me the study's central methodological problem. My proposal treats that as the defining challenge rather than a caveat, and specifies independent recruitment and interviewing, anonymised transcripts, an independent gatekeeper with authority to halt the study, and an external critical friend whose dissent is recorded in the thesis.

In support of the discretionary provision I am submitting, or will have completed prior to enrolment:

- a **critical commentary** on *Multiply or Die* of 12,000–15,000 words, situating the book in the church multiplication, organisational studies and leadership development literatures, classifying the evidential basis of each of its propositions, and stating its limitations;
- a **peer-reviewed journal article** derived from the network's documented church plant failure, submitted to *Australasian Pentecostal Studies*;
- completion of **RES701** or an equivalent research methodology unit;
- a full academic curriculum vitae and the research proposal.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss the proposal, and in particular to hear whether the Graduate Research School considers the Doctor of Ministry or the Master of Philosophy the more appropriate point of entry. I have no attachment to the faster route. I would rather begin at the level the evidence supports and earn the rest.

My connection to Alphacrucis is long-standing — Futures College operates as a delivery partner, and I hold the University's honorary doctorate. I would value doing this work within an institution whose tradition and commitments I share, and whose faculty understand the context I am researching from the inside.

Thank you for your consideration.

Yours sincerely,

**Ashley Mark Evans**
Global Senior Pastor, Futures Church Global
