# Referees, Documents and Timeline

---

## 1. Referees

AC may request **confidential referee reports from two people who can comment on your writing skills and research potential**. Note the specific wording — *writing* and *research potential*, not character or ministry impact. A glowing reference about your leadership that says nothing about how you think on paper is a wasted slot.

### Who to choose

| Slot | Profile | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **An academic** — ideally AC faculty, or a scholar who has read your written work seriously | The only referee who can speak to research potential in terms the committee weights. Non-negotiable; find one even if you must build the relationship first. |
| 2 | **A senior movement leader** — denominational or national office, with a doctorate themselves if possible | Can speak to standing and to sustained engagement with the subject over decades. A referee who holds a research degree understands what is being asked. |
| 3 | **Governance / board chair** *(if a third is permitted)* | Speaks to the organisational access and the ethics of researching your own network — the reviewer will want reassurance that the study has institutional backing. |

### What to send each referee

Send a brief, and send it early. Referees write better reports when they know what is being assessed:

- The research proposal (document 02)
- Your academic CV (document 03)
- A short note stating: *"AC has asked referees to comment specifically on writing skills and research potential. If you are able to speak to how I handle evidence, argument and criticism, that will be more useful than comment on ministry outcomes."*
- The deadline, with two weeks' buffer

## 2. Document checklist

### Required for application

- [ ] Expression of Interest submitted (document 06)
- [ ] AC's written response on the discretionary provision received and read
- [ ] Application for admission form completed — [formsite link](https://fs25.formsite.com/AlphacrucisCollege/uymmqruxfl/index.html)
- [ ] Cover letter (document 04) — finalised with correct addressee
- [ ] Research proposal (document 02) — reformatted to AC's specified length
- [ ] Academic CV (document 03) — **all `[CONFIRM]` placeholders resolved**
- [ ] **Certified academic transcript** — bachelor's degree. *Order this now; institutional turnaround is often 4–8 weeks and it is the most common cause of missed deadlines.*
- [ ] Certified copy of degree certificate
- [ ] Proof of identity / citizenship
- [ ] Two referee reports arranged
- [ ] *Multiply or Die* — physical copy plus PDF

### Strengthening pack — probably optional, worth doing

*Not required on AC's published DMin criteria. Deploy if AC's answer to question 1 calls for it, or simply to make the application unarguable.*

- [ ] **Critical commentary, 12,000–15,000 words** (document 05)
- [ ] Peer-reviewed article submitted to *Australasian Pentecostal Studies*
- [ ] RES701 or equivalent research methods unit — enrolled or completed
- [ ] Publication list with extents and publishers verified
- [ ] Statement distinguishing the honorary doctorate from academic qualifications *(handled in the cover letter — make sure no other document blurs it)*

### Before candidature begins

- [ ] Supervisor conversations held
- [ ] Ethics pre-consultation with AC's HREC
- [ ] Independent researcher identified and costed (interviews + transcription)
- [ ] Independent gatekeeper appointed and briefed
- [ ] External critical friend approached
- [ ] Board resolution authorising organisational access to archives and staff
- [ ] Archive audit — confirm 2000–2026 records are actually retrievable, in all four countries

> **That last item deserves a flag.** The entire proposal rests on the claim that a complete 26-year archive exists and is accessible. Verify it before you promise it. If the Indonesian or Brazilian records are patchy, better to know now and scope the study honestly than to discover it in year two.

## 3. Timeline

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **This week** | Submit EOI. Order academic transcript. Confirm bachelor's details. Resolve CV placeholders. |
| **Weeks 2–4** | AC response — especially question 1. Approach referees. Finalise CV, cover letter and proposal. |
| **Weeks 4–8** | **Submit the application.** If AC's answer requires the strengthening pack, hold and proceed below instead. |
| **Weeks 4–8 (parallel)** | Enrol in RES701 if available. Begin proposition extraction. Request library access. |
| **Months 2–4** | Literature survey. Draft critical commentary. Draft journal article. |
| **Months 4–6** | Complete commentary. Internal review. Submit article. |
| **2027** | Commence candidature. Confirmation of candidature falls within 6–12 months FTE of the research component starting. |

**The point of this sequence:** don't hold the application hostage to the strengthening pack. Apply on the criteria AC publishes; build the commentary alongside. If AC comes back asking for more, you are already three months into producing it.

## 5. Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| AC applies a research-output test to DMin entry not shown on its course page | Moderate | Confirm at EOI stage — question 1 in document 06. If yes, deploy the strengthening pack |
| AC declines DMin entry | Low | Published criteria are experience-based and you exceed them eightfold. MPhil fallback pre-negotiated in the same conversation (document 01, Route B) |
| Archive incomplete in some countries | Moderate | Audit before submission; scope the study to what genuinely exists |
| Departed leaders decline to participate | High | Expected. Independent approach; report non-participation transparently as a limitation |
| Ethics approval delayed by insider-research complexity | High | Early pre-consultation; do not treat HREC as a formality |
| Time pressure from the day job | High | Part-time candidature; fieldwork embedded in existing travel; realistic 6-year plan |
| Findings damaging to the network | Moderate | Gatekeeper and supervisory panel share the publication decision — by design, it is not yours alone |
