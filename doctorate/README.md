## Read this first

**The book alone will not get you in — but it is the raw material for a strong application.**

*Multiply or Die* is not peer-reviewed, states no methodology, and does not test its own claims. Your honorary doctorate from Alphacrucis is recognition, not academic standing, and does not count toward entry. With a bachelor's degree and no honours thesis or masters, you do not currently meet AC's standard prerequisite — a prior research thesis of **≥12,000 words**.

**There is a real door.** AC's rules allow admission in exceptional circumstances, on the recommendation of the Graduate Research School Director, for applicants who demonstrate equivalent research training through peer-reviewed publications, professional research outputs, or other verifiable evidence of research capacity. That provision exists for practitioners like you. This pack is built to satisfy it on the evidence rather than ask for an exception.

**The framing that makes it work:**

> **The book is the hypothesis. The doctorate is the test.**

You published a set of laws about multiplication drawn from four decades of practice and 21+ church plants. You asserted them; you never tested them. A study that subjects them to systematic comparative scrutiny — across the full population of your plants in four countries, *including the ones that failed* — is a genuine contribution, and you may be the only person alive positioned to do it.

That framing is also the answer to the sharpest objection an examiner will raise, which is why the proposal leads with it.

## The recommended route

**Doctor of Ministry via discretionary admission**, supported by:

1. A **12,000–15,000-word critical commentary** on *Multiply or Die* — the standard instrument in PhD-by-published-work applications, and the artefact that does the heavy lifting
2. A **peer-reviewed article** submitted to *Australasian Pentecostal Studies* — on the church plant that **failed**, because leading with the failure demonstrates exactly the disposition being assessed
3. **RES701** or equivalent research methods training
4. **40 years of practice** against a 5-year requirement

**Fallback:** MPhil under the same provision, upgrading to PhD on good progress. Pre-negotiate this in the same conversation.

**Realistic timeline:** 4–6 months of part-time work to a complete application; enrolment in 2027.

## One caution

Everything here depends on a claim the proposal makes repeatedly: that a **complete 2000–2026 archive exists and is accessible across all four countries**. Audit that before you promise it. If the Indonesian or Brazilian records are patchy, far better to scope the study honestly now than to discover the gap in year two of candidature.
